Chapter 01

Mechanical Engineering

MET, Nashik | Class of 2015

I studied Mechanical Engineering at MET, Nashik, graduating in 2015.

Those four years exposed me to quite a few things, from engineering drawings and workshops to robotics, manufacturing processes and industrial systems. There was a lot of theory, of course, but I was always more interested in seeing how things actually worked.

I enjoyed working with machines, understanding mechanisms and figuring out why something wasn't behaving the way it was supposed to.

Looking back, some of my most memorable experiences happened outside regular lectures.

Robotics, Projects & Competitions

Robotics was one of the areas I explored during engineering.

I participated in technical competitions, including ROBOTHON, where I won a robotics race competition. It was an opportunity to work with mechanical systems beyond what we were studying in classrooms.

Those projects also exposed me to the practical side of engineering, where designing something and getting it to work are two different experiences.

Bhabha Atomic Research Centre

Tarapur | 2013

During my engineering years, I had an opportunity to undergo training at the Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC), Tarapur, in the PREFRE division (Processing and Fabrication of Reactor Materials).

My work involved studying industrial utility systems, particularly boilers and cooling systems, and understanding their operating principles and efficiency.

It was an early exposure to engineering at an industrial scale, where equipment reliability, operating conditions and processes had practical significance.

Bosch India

Nashik | 2014-2015

I subsequently completed my engineering training at Bosch, Nashik.

My work involved studying industrial utility systems, including boilers, chillers and cooling systems, assessing their efficiency and preparing technical reports.

It gave me further exposure to how engineering systems operate within a large manufacturing environment.

Beyond Academics

College wasn't entirely about engineering.

I was also involved in technical events, quizzes and sports. Those experiences were another part of my time at MET, alongside the practical work and academic requirements.

Moving Into Manufacturing

I graduated in 2015 and began my professional career in manufacturing.

My first role was at Bharat Flooring & Tiles, where I became involved in production processes, quality systems and operational improvements.

The transition from studying manufacturing to working in an actual production environment gave me a much closer view of what happens on a factory floor.

That became the next chapter of my career.
